# Cursor ideas

The backlog of cursors to build, one at a time. Names stay generic; the reference lives in the behaviour, never in a trademarked name. Tick an idea off when it ships, and move it to the changelog.

Want to build one? Pick an unticked idea, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), and your name goes on its page.

## Sci-fi

- [x] **Saber**: a glowing energy blade that leaves streaks as you swing. It ignites when the pointer arrives, guards over controls, clashes in sparks on a click, and powers down when still.
- [ ] **Warp**: stars streak out from the pointer as you speed up. Press to jump: everything stretches into lines, then drops back.
- [ ] **Beam**: hold and the pointer dissolves into a column of sparkles; let go and it rematerialises.
- [ ] **Hologram**: a flickering blue scanline projection that glitches as you move and projects a holo-frame around controls.
- [ ] **Droid**: a round robot rolling after you, beeping in speech bubbles, its head turning toward controls.
- [ ] **Portal**: clicks place a blue and an orange portal; move into one and you come out of the other.

## Lenses that bend the page

These reuse Black hole's technique: an SVG displacement map applied as a `backdrop-filter`.

- [x] **Magnifier**: a real magnifying glass that enlarges the page under it.
- [x] **Droplet**: a water drop that refracts the page; clicks splash ripples that bend the text.
- [x] **Glitch**: an RGB split and sliced displacement of the page under the pointer, harder over controls.
- [ ] **Heat**: shimmering heat haze above a flame; the page wavers like a desert road.

## Fantasy and myth

- [x] **Wand**: spell sparks trail the tip. Draw a circle to cast a shield; click to cast a spark.
- [x] **Dragon**: a small dragon flies beside you; clicks breathe fire and leave fading scorch marks.
- [ ] **Bow**: hold to draw, release to fire an arrow that sticks into the hovered control and wobbles.
- [ ] **Fairy**: pixie dust trails behind; controls get sprinkled and glow.
- [ ] **Rewind**: an hourglass. Press and your last two seconds of movement replay backwards as a ghost.

## Games

- [x] **Laser**: the pointer is a laser dot and a cat chases it, pounces on clicks and naps when you stop.
- [x] **Coin**: each click pops a spinning coin with "+1" and a running score.
- [ ] **Blocks**: a falling block piece that snaps onto controls, which flash like a cleared line.
- [ ] **Scope**: a crosshair that locks onto controls; clicks show hit markers and damage numbers.
- [ ] **Loot**: controls glow by rarity and show an item card with made-up stats.
- [ ] **Shuriken**: clicks throw a spinning star that sticks into whatever you hit.

## Everyday, festive and places

- [x] **Fireworks**: clicks launch a shell that bursts; hold for a bigger one.
- [ ] **Lighthouse**: a sweeping beam that locks onto controls, with fog drifting through it.
- [x] **Fireflies**: a swarm that follows you and settles on controls; brighter in dark mode.
- [ ] **Vinyl**: a spinning record whose needle drops over controls; dragging scratches it.
- [ ] **Polaroid**: clicks flash and drop a photo frame that slowly develops.
- [x] **Lantern**: festival lanterns (Diwali, Lunar New Year) that rise and drift off on each click.
- [x] **Fountain pen**: writes your path in ink of varying width and underlines controls. Shipped as Pen.

## Materials and nature

- [x] **Mercury**: a bead of liquid metal that sheds droplets when you move fast; a click bursts it and the drops roll home.
- [x] **Halftone**: a printer's halftone screen round the pointer that turns its angle over controls and spreads its ink while held.
- [x] **Leaves**: autumn leaves that tumble off your path and pile up on buttons; a click is a gust.
- [x] **Smoke**: an ember with curling smoke; a click blows a smoke ring.
- [ ] **Ink in water**: a drop of ink blooming into clouds where you click, slowly diffusing.
- [x] **Sand**: grains that pour from the pointer and pile into dunes along the bottom of the area.

## Showpieces

- [x] **Galaxy**: a spiral galaxy turning round the pointer; its stars trace a hovered control's outline and go supernova on press.
- [x] **Liquid**: a drop of liquid glass that bends the page at its rim and flows out to wrap a hovered control.
- [x] **Gecko**: a procedurally animated lizard that stalks the pointer, steps in diagonal pairs and flicks its tongue at controls.
- [x] **Fluid**: dye in a real fluid simulation, stirred by the pointer and splashed by clicks (WebGL).
- [ ] **Cloth**: a flag of real cloth hanging from the pointer, waving the hovered control's name.
